# Math3201Review

Math 3201 review and practice app: pick units or indicators, set the count and level mix, practise online or print a sheet with a worked answer key.

Single-file interactive built for the Academic Mathematics 3201 review project. Covers all nine units. The Mid-course preset covers Units 1 to 4 at the published mid-course blueprint mix (20 / 62 / 18), and the Final review preset covers Units 5 to 9 at 20 / 60 / 20, the proportions of the most recent published final blueprint.

Status: draft, under construction. The chassis and curriculum skeleton (units, indicators, presets, level mixes) are in place and verified; the question bank is being authored from the ExamView coverage map, mid-course units first, and is currently empty, so the app has no questions to serve yet. Not yet listed in the CDLI applications index.
